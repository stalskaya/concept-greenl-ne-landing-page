Mowing season runs March to November.

San Antonio grass grows nine months a year, so the schedule changes with it. Tap a month to see what happens on your lawn.

JanuaryOff season · 1 visit

The lawn is dormant. One cleanup visit: leaves, fallen branches, beds raked out.

FebruaryOff season · 1 visit

Pre-emergent goes down in the second half of the month, before the soil warms and the spring weeds sprout. Ornamental grasses get cut back.

MarchEvery two weeks

First cuts as the lawn greens up. Live oaks drop their old leaves now too, so March cleanups take a little longer.

AprilWeekly

Weekly mowing starts. The first feeding goes down once the lawn is fully green and has been cut twice.

MayWeekly

Fastest growth of the spring. Edges on every visit, beds pulled back from the walk.

JuneWeekly

We raise the mower deck for the heat. Taller grass shades its own roots and holds water longer between your watering days.

JulyWeekly

Summer height, early starts. We check St. Augustine lawns for chinch bug patches and text you a photo if we find one.

AugustWeekly

The hardest month on a lawn. We don't cut a lawn short while it's stressed from the heat, even if the yard looks a bit shaggy for a few weeks.

SeptemberWeekly

Growth picks up again once the heat breaks. Fall feeding goes down late in the month.

OctoberWeekly, then every two weeks

Growth slows. Fall pre-emergent for winter weeds. We drop to every two weeks when the lawn stops keeping up with a weekly cut.

NovemberEvery two weeks

Last regular cuts. Pecans and cedar elms start dropping, so leaves get mulched or hauled on every visit.

DecemberOff season · 1 visit

Final leaf haul of the year. The lawn goes dormant and visits drop to one a month until February.

Every visit runs in the same order.

Same crew every week, same steps every time. A medium yard takes about forty minutes from the gate to the photo.

Two people, one truckYour crew lead is the person you text. If the regular crew is out, you hear about it the day before.

The visit

Medium yard · about 40 min
  1. Heads upA text when we're 30 minutes out, so the dog can go inside and the gate can be unlocked.
  2. MowSt. Augustine at 3.5 to 4 inches, Bermuda lower. The direction changes each week so the grass stands up straight.
  3. EdgeDriveway, walks and curb get a clean vertical edge.
  4. TrimFence lines, tree trunks, the mailbox post and around the AC unit.
  5. Blow offClippings go back onto the lawn, not into the street or the storm drain.
  6. PhotoA picture of the finished yard lands in your texts before the truck leaves.
